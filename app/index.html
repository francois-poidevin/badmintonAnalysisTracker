Badminton analysis tracker

How to use (everything runs in your browser; the video never leaves your computer)
  1. Video: choose a match clip. Best results: fixed camera, whole court visible, 30 fps, a few minutes at most. It is kept in the browser storage, so it is still there after a reload.
  2. Engines (see choices below), then Weight (used for calories only; changing it later updates the table at once, no re-analysis).
  3. Court: click the 4 court corners on the picture, in this order: far-left, far-right, near-right, near-left (outer lines, doubles court). The corners are used to convert pixels into metres.
  4. Analyse: runs the models on every frame (slow, depends on your GPU; you see what the models detect live). Cancel stops it and discards the partial result.
  5. Preview AR: replays the video with the overlay (court lines, skeletons, player ids and colours, shuttle with tail and speed, mini-map with player paths, distance and kcal table). It can be replayed as often as you want; no new computation.
  6. Export AR mp4: encodes the same overlay into a downloadable MP4 (video only, no sound).

Engines - what each choice means

  • Pose: RTMPose-s + YOLOX-tiny (default, Apache-2.0): two steps, first find each person, then estimate the 17 body points. More accurate on small, far players; slower; the first run also downloads about 40 MB. YOLOv8n-pose: one step, faster, usually less precise on distant players, and its licence (AGPL) is restrictive if you reuse it in a product. Bad pose = wrong feet position, hence wrong distance, calories and map.
  • Shuttle: TrackNetV3 (default, 45 MB download): finds the shuttle in every frame; needed for the shuttle tail and km/h. It reads 8 frames at a time and builds an empty-court background first, so it works best with a fixed camera. off: no shuttle, no speed, but analysis is faster (only the frames selected by "every N" are read).
  • Analyse every N frames (pose only): 1 = most precise, slowest. 2-5 = faster; the frames in between are interpolated, so quick movements are smoothed (distance may be slightly underestimated). Shuttle detection always uses every frame when it is on.

Court corners: the accuracy of every metre value (positions, distance, calories, shuttle km/h) depends on these 4 clicks. Click precisely on the outer line intersections. Wrong order or a shifted corner gives a mirrored/skewed map and wrong distances. Only people inside the court or within 0.6 m of it are tracked (spectators and referees are ignored). The map shows the far end at the top, as in the corner order above.

Reset: clears the 4 corners and the analysis results (preview and export become unavailable). The video stays loaded. You must click the corners again and re-run Analyse. Use it if a corner was misplaced, or the camera moved. To only change weight, no reset is needed.

Speeds and kcal are estimates: the shuttle speed is measured on the court plane (overestimates when the shuttle is high); calories use average METs by player speed.
